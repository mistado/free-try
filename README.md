# 👕 AI Outfit Change Tool

An AI-powered outfit change tool that lets you **change clothes in a photo while keeping the original person, pose, and overall appearance**.

Upload an image, describe the outfit you want, and let AI generate a new look in seconds.

## ✨ Features

- 👗 **Change Outfits with AI** — Replace clothing using a simple text prompt.
- 🧥 **Multiple Clothing Styles** — Try casual wear, business attire, dresses, suits, streetwear, uniforms, costumes, and more.
- 🧍 **Preserve the Person** — Designed to maintain the person's face, body, pose, and identity while changing their clothes.
- 🎨 **Prompt-Based Editing** — Describe exactly what you want, such as:
  - `Black business suit with a white shirt`
  - `Red evening dress`
  - `Casual oversized hoodie and jeans`
  - `Summer beach outfit`
- ⚡ **Fast Generation** — Create outfit variations in just a few steps.
- 📱 **Easy to Use** — No professional photo-editing experience required.

## 🚀 How It Works

1. Upload a photo of a person.
2. Select or describe the desired outfit.
3. Start the AI generation.
4. Preview the result.
5. Save your favorite version.

## 🛠️ Usage

Open the Outfit Change Tool, upload your image, and enter a description of the clothes you want.

For best results:

- Use a clear, high-quality image.
- Make sure the person's clothing is visible.
- Describe the desired clothing specifically.
- Include details such as color, material, style, and accessories when necessary.

For example:

```text
Replace the current clothes with a dark blue slim-fit suit,
white dress shirt, black leather belt, and black formal shoes.
```

## 🔒 Privacy

Please avoid uploading images that you do not have permission to use.

Uploaded images should be handled according to the privacy policy of the service hosting the tool.

## ⚠️ Disclaimer

AI-generated results may not always be accurate. Clothing details, body proportions, hands, accessories, patterns, and other elements may occasionally be generated incorrectly.

This tool is intended for creative and legitimate image-editing purposes.
